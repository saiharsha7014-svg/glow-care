Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Live Face Skin Scanner medically certified? ▼
No. Glow Care provides general skincare guidance and educational lifestyle recommendations only. The scanner utilizes browser computer vision to analyze visible skin characteristics such as surface oiliness, redness, and texture contrast. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose dermatological diseases.
Does Glow Care store my camera video or pictures? ▼
Never. All image processing and pixel analysis are performed entirely client-side inside your browser’s memory. No video stream or biometric frames are transmitted to remote servers.
Can I reschedule or cancel a booked appointment? ▼
Yes! You can view and cancel any scheduled appointment at any time by visiting the "My Bookings" page.
How are personalized products recommended? ▼
Our recommendation algorithm matches the primary skin concerns detected during your scan (e.g. acne, dryness, oily shine, dark spots) with safe, non-comedogenic active formulations designed to address those specific concerns.
