Science Meets Aesthetics

Skin Intelligence Redefined

Glow Care is designed to demystify skin health. By combining browser computer vision with non-comedogenic dermatological science, we provide personalized guidance that empowers every individual.

Our Mission

Accessible, Private, and Science-Backed Skincare

Traditional skin questionnaires often rely on guesswork. Glow Care leverages high-performance browser computer vision to analyze actual visible skin characteristics directly through your webcam—without storing or transmitting private biometric data.

We do not claim to diagnose diseases or medical conditions. Instead, we bridge the gap between self-care and professional guidance with transparent, general skincare recommendations.

Our Philosophy

The Three Pillars of Glow Care

🔒

100% Client-Side Privacy

All pixel matrix calculations, face alignment geometry, and tone assessments occur locally in your browser sandbox using HTML5 Canvas & WebGL. Your video feed is never uploaded to the cloud.

🌿

Barrier-First Skincare

We focus on lipid barrier repair with ceramides, panthenol, and hyaluronic acid. We never prescribe harsh drugs or unproven synthetic additives.

💡

Dermatology Alignment

Our algorithms are calibrated against recognized aesthetic scales (Fitzpatrick phototypes, Sebum shine index, and Glogau classification) to ensure relevant, helpful suggestions.
